Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Clenfra ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Clenfra mobile application (the "App"), which connects users with verified legal professionals for consultations, legal advice, and case management. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number when you create an account.
  • Content you provide: documents, images, messages, and case details you share with your lawyer through the App.
  • Communications: voice and video call activity and related call records used to deliver and maintain the service.
  • Camera and microphone: accessed only during voice and video calls, with your permission.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app performance, crash reports, and usage information to help us improve the App.
  • Device identifiers: used to deliver notifications and keep your account secure.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the App, including matching you with lawyers and enabling consultations.
  • To process and track your legal requests and case activity.
  • To send service-related notifications, such as meeting reminders and message alerts.
  • To maintain security, prevent fraud, and improve app reliability and performance.

Sharing of Information

We share your information only with the verified lawyer(s) assigned to your request, and with service providers who help us operate the App (such as cloud hosting and messaging infrastructure). We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for third-party advertising or tracking.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the email below. You can also delete your account from within the App.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
